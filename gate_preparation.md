# 🧠 Your study strategy

For every subject, use this cycle:

### 1. Learn

Watch/read a free lecture/resource.

### 2. Make short notes

Not 100 pages of notes.

Make notes you can revise in **30–60 minutes**.

### 3. Solve questions immediately

Don't wait until finishing the entire subject.

### 4. Solve PYQs

This is extremely important.

### 5. Record mistakes

Maintain a:

> **GATE Error Notebook**

For every mistake:

```text
Subject:
Topic:
Question:
Why did I get it wrong?
Correct concept:
How will I recognize this next time?
```

This notebook becomes incredibly valuable in January.

January becomes your **exam simulation month**.

You'll gradually move toward:

### Full-length tests

↓

### Analyze test

↓

### Identify weak topics

↓

### Revise

↓

### Another test

The test itself isn't the important part.

**Analysis is.**

Suppose you get:

```text
Score: 62
```

Don't just say:

> "I need 80."

Instead classify every wrong question:

```text
Silly mistake       → 5
Conceptual gap      → 8
Forgot formula      → 3
Time pressure       → 2
Didn't understand   → 4
```

Now you know exactly what to fix.
